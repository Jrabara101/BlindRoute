How BlindRoute Works

STEP 1 OF 5

Connect Your Wallet

Click Connect Wallet on the BlindRoute page. Lace opens a popup — here's exactly what to expect and tap through:

1. Lace asks you to authorize BlindRoute. Click Select Account first.

2. Pick an account, then click Authorize.

3. If locked, enter your password and click Confirm.

Before any of this: make sure Lace is set to the Preview network with some test funds (tDUST) from the Preview faucet.

STEP 2 OF 5

Deploy or Join a Contract

Click Initialize to start a fresh escrow (you're the payer) — or paste an existing contract address and click Verify & Join to connect to someone else's escrow instead.

Either action opens a Lace popup like this — review the transaction data, then click Sign transaction. (Same confirmation reappears for Lock, Release, and Reclaim.)

STEP 3 OF 5

Lock the Escrow

As the payer, set an Amount and a Refund Deadline (in ticks), then click Lock Escrow.

1. A delivery secret is hashed locally in your browser — this can take a little while.

2. Lace confirms — shows entry_point: lockEscrow. Click Sign transaction.

Only a hash of the delivery secret goes on-chain — the secret itself never leaves your browser.

STEP 4 OF 5

Escrow Locked

Funds are now cryptographically secured. Three actions become available:

  • • Release Escrow (Courier) — proves the secret is known and claims the funds.
  • • Reclaim Funds (Payer) — usable after the refund deadline passes.
  • • Advance Clock — anyone can advance the shared tick counter.

Clicking Release Escrow opens this confirmation — shows entry_point: releaseEscrow.

STEP 5 OF 5

Release (Courier) or Reclaim (Payer)

Release proves — in zero knowledge, right in your browser — that the courier knows the secret. This step is slower than locking; that's expected. The status flips to RELEASED and the Public Ledger State card updates, while Private Session never reveals the secret.

Once you release (or reclaim), this Quick Feedback form pops up automatically — or click Feedback in the top bar anytime.

Ready to try it? Close this guide and connect your wallet.
